# Brand Guidelines

Visual and verbal identity for MovieAIapp. **Not yet defined in Figma or code** — initial direction below for discovery; replace with finalized tokens when design exploration completes.

## Brand personality

- **Smart, not smug** — knows film, doesn't lecture
- **Warm, cinematic** — evening-at-home energy, not neon tech-bro
- **Decisive** — helps you act, not endless browse

## Color (proposed direction)

Dark-first UI suits movie posters and living-room use.

| Token | Proposed value | Use |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| Background | Near-black `#0D0D0F` | App shell |
| Surface | `#1A1A1F` | Cards, chat bubbles |
| Accent | Warm amber or coral TBD | CTAs, active chips |
| Text primary | `#F5F5F7` | Headlines, body |
| Text muted | `#8E8E93` | Metadata, timestamps |

## Spacing & grid

- Base unit: **4px**
- Screen padding: **16px** mobile, **24px** tablet+
- Card gap: **12px**
- Poster aspect ratio: **2:3** standard

## Tone of voice

- Second person ("you"), concise sentences
- Avoid hype ("mind-blowing AI") — show usefulness through picks and rationale
- Example: *"Try a tight sci-fi mystery — under two hours, slow burn, no jump scares."*
