# Users & Personas

Working hypotheses until user research is run. Validate via interviews or a lightweight survey — findings go in [research-notes.md](../prd/research-notes.md).

## Primary persona (hypothesis): The Decider

**Who:** Adult streamer, 25–45, 2+ subscriptions, watches 3–8 hrs/week.

**Goal:** Pick something good within 10 minutes without scrolling three apps.

**Pain points:**
- Recommendation rows feel repetitive or obviously sponsored
- Can't express mood or constraints in natural language inside streaming apps
- Forgets what's on their watchlist or what's newly available on services they pay for

**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me choose tonight's movie"
- "Tell me where I can watch this"
- "Surprise me, but not something I'll hate"

## Secondary persona (hypothesis): The Enthusiast

**Who:** Film fan who logs watches on Letterboxd or similar.

**Goal:** Discover lesser-known titles aligned with specific directors, eras, or themes.

**Pain points:**
- Discovery tools are either social (Letterboxd) or catalog-browse (JustWatch), rarely conversational
- AI chat gives titles but not structured cards, trailers, or availability

**Jobs to be done:**
- "Find me something in the vein of X that I haven't seen"
- "Build a themed list for a watch party"

## Anti-persona (for v1 scope)

- **Power curator** who wants full list management, social feeds, and review publishing — out of scope until core pick-and-watch loop works.
- **Piracy-focused user** — not a target; product assumes legal streaming/rental paths.
