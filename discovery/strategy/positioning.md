# Positioning

How MovieAIapp should be understood in the market. Draft until validated with users.

## One-liner (working)

**MovieAIapp helps you decide what to watch tonight — using AI that understands your mood, taste, and what's actually available to stream.**

## Target category

AI-native movie discovery — not a streaming service, not a social network, not a generic chatbot.

## Key differentiators

1. **Built for the decision** — narrow focus on choosing one title, not browsing endless catalogs.
2. **Explains the pick** — short, spoiler-safe rationale so users trust the suggestion.
3. **Availability-aware** (planned) — recommendations respect region and subscriptions where data allows.

## Messaging pillars

| Pillar | Message |
|--------|---------|
| **Clarity** | "Stop scrolling. Start watching." |
| **Taste** | "Recommendations that sound like a friend who knows film." |
| **Action** | "See where to stream it in one tap." |

## What we are not claiming (yet)

- "Best AI" or model-specific branding — provider TBD
- Comprehensive catalog of every film ever made — depends on API coverage
- Replacement for Letterboxd-style social graph
